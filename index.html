Official Legal Guidelines

OUR TERMS AND CONDITIONS

I ABOUT THE GOODS

  • a)
    You will be informed of the departure day and date from the USA to Cameroon. However, we shall not be liable for any delays that may occur due to circumstances beyond our control.
  • b)
    You will be informed as soon as the ship arrives Douala - Cameroon.
  • c)
    You will be informed when the customs clearing process is completed and the exact day and date the container will be out.
  • d)
    You will be informed of the offloading day and date, place of offloading and time.
  • e)
    Once offloaded, you will be expected to verify the state and content of your consignment before entering full possession.
  • f)
    If by mistake or misunderstanding items that do not belong to you are found in your consignment, you will be bound to refund them without delay.
  • g)
    Once you leave the offloading place after completing verification of your consignment, the delivery process is considered effective and completed.

II LOST OR DAMAGED ITEMS

  • a)
    Starlight Shipping will pay a maximum of $150 to any confirmed damaged uninsured items.
  • b)
    To receive any payment for damaged uninsured cargo, the claimant must establish a prima facie that the goods were delivered to the carrier in good condition and were discharged in a damaged state.
  • c)
    Starlight Shipping will pay a maximum of $500 to any confirmed missing/lost uninsured items as determined by the sender and Starlight.
  • d)
    To receive payment for a loss, the shipper must establish a prima facie case by showing the goods were delivered to Starlight in good condition and were either not delivered or delivered damaged.
  • e)
    Starlight would not pay for a loss caused by either Perils of the Sea, Act of God/Natural disasters, Error in Navigation, or Insufficient Packing.
  • f)
    Starlight would pay the commercial value for lost or damaged insured items. Insurance pay is 10% of the declared value of the items at the time of shipment.

III ABOUT THE CUSTOM DUTIES

  • a)
    The amount charged for each item will depend on the quantity and value of the item.
  • b)
    Once You are informed of the amount you are required to pay for your consignment. Payment must be made in full before the container is removed by the clearing agent.
  • c)
    If for one Reason or another you fail to pay on the agreed day, date and time, you will be required to pay an extra 10 percent of the total amount.
  • d)
    You will enter complete possession of your items only after full payment is made.
  • e)
    You have four maximum days to pick up your items from our warehouse after the container is offloaded after which a daily storage fee of 2500 francs will apply.
